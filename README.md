# Attack Round - Vanilla Edition ⚔️

Welcome to Attack Round! This is a browser-based battle game built with vanilla JavaScript, HTML, and CSS.

## About

Attack Round is an interactive turn-based combat game where you face off against an enemy in an epic battle. Use your strategic skills to choose between attacking, defending, or unleashing special attacks to defeat your opponent!

## Features

- **Turn-based Combat**: Strategic gameplay with player and enemy turns
- **Multiple Actions**: 
  - ⚔️ **Attack**: Deal damage to your enemy
  - 🛡️ **Defend**: Reduce incoming damage on the next attack
  - ✨ **Special Attack**: Unleash a powerful attack (with cooldown)
- **Dynamic Health Bars**: Visual representation of health status
- **Battle Log**: Track all actions during the battle
- **Responsive Design**: Works on desktop and mobile devices
- **Pure Vanilla JavaScript**: No frameworks or dependencies required

## How to Play

1. Open `index.html` in your web browser
2. Choose your action each round:
   - Click "Attack" to deal damage to the enemy
   - Click "Defend" to reduce damage from the next enemy attack
   - Click "Special Attack" for a powerful attack (available every 3 rounds)
3. Watch the battle unfold in the battle log
4. Win by reducing the enemy's HP to zero!
5. Click "New Game" to start over

## Installation

No installation required! Simply:

1. Clone or download this repository
2. Open `index.html` in any modern web browser
3. Start playing!
